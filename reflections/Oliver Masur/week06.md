## Reflection
This exercise was a nice AI Add on, because up until now we didnt learn about AI at all.  

**Expectations & Takeaways**
I expected to learn a little bit about AI, which actually I did in a pretty nice and interactive way.   

**What was good vs. difficult**
It was good that we didn't need any additional hardware except our smartphones so we could go straight to learning and collecting data, just by scanning a QR code and shaking our phones. Felt a little bit stupid to rotate your phone in a circle for over 3 minutes straight, but it was fun and we collected some data to work with. Also the EdgeImpulse dashboard is built pretty simple. The workload was already pretty high due to the project but we decided to complete the tasks in this module anyway. One thing that didn't work was that on my old iPhone 12 I set up 10 seconds as a record time span and it recorded for 10 seconds but the dashboard received only 5 seconds. So I had to re collect the data with another phone.

**Interactions & Performance**
 We split up the motion gestures so everyone took part in collecting data and also the tasks were split up nicely. Very good communication in the team and a fun module.
