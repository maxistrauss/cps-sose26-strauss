## Reflection

**Expectations & Takeaways**
Going into this first week, my main expectation was really just to get the organizational overhead out of the way—finding a team, setting up Git, and getting the Arduino hardware sorted. My biggest takeaway from the research part was realizing how blurry the line gets between a pure Cyber-Physical System and a IoT system. 

**What was good vs. difficult**
It was good that we got the hardware distribution handled immediately, so we have the components ready to go for the upcoming practical labs. On the other hand, a negative point was the Git setup. Everyone ended up creating their own individual repository, which honestly feels pretty inefficient. In my opinion, it would have made much more sense to just create one central team repository right from the start to streamline collaboration.

**Interactions & Performance**
Team formation went smoothly. We got a solid group together and coordinated well to split up the initial research questions. I think we have a decent foundation for the actual coding ahead.
