## Reflection
For this week, we had no lecture and only the exercise to do. Everyone in the team dealth with a specific task. My task was exercise 1, moduling an example of a CPS architecture for a specific application and think about if ML is actually needed for parts of the architecture or if model-based approaches might be sufficient. 

**Expectations & Takeaways**
I expected a difficult exercise, which goes into depth off CPS and Advanced IoT. A big takeaway was that often it doesn't really make sense to overengineer systems with ML/AI and rule-based architectures are sometimes the better and more robust choice. Especially in medical applications, explainability of a decision is an important factor and transparent thresholds might be better in some cases.

**What was good vs. difficult**
ChatGPT was a good starting point to find out how "Best-Practice" Edge IoT devices look like in medical fields. At first, I didn't really know where to start but it helped a lot to have an example architecture which I could optimize.
