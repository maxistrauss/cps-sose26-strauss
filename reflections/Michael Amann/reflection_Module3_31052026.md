### About the exercise

#### Positives

The exercise was very nicely structured so that we first had to complete a couple of exercises to get to know the used frameworks like IoTempower and Node-Red. After understanding the basics of those frameworks, we were able to complete the mini-Project fairly easily. It was also very nice, that even though we had core features, that we had to implement, we also had some freedom to add our personal ideas. This allowed us to be creative and not just repeat instructions from some tutorial. 

#### Struggles

We had some struggles when flashing the devices with iotempower, as the raspberry PI had so little RAM. After installing IoTempower to my personal PC, this problem was solved. We also had some struggles with Wifi connectivity of our Arduino, so we had to use an ESP32.
