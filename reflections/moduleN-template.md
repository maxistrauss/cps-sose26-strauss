## Individual reflection on weekNN / moduleN (lecture and/or lab)

- What were you expectations of the class / lecture / lab session?
- What is your “takeaway”, what do you want to remember, what would be your advice? “Takeaway” can be a super short summary, but better a list of points that stand out for you.
- What was good?
- What was difficult? Where did you struggle?
- Was there any good/fun “struggling”/exploration? Any show stoppers – anything where you should reach out to peers or instructors?
- How do you evaluate your own performance?
- How was your interaction with peers/instructors? Did you work well with your peer(s), did you have to work a lot alone, could you have done something better yourself to improve work with your peers?
- Help and extra work (this is important to gather potential extra points)
  - Who helped you, gave feedback, was it valuable?
  - Who did you help, give feedback to?
  - Did you present, implement, make a PR, or fix something that was crucial for the class in itself?
