## MQTT basics, barebone MQTT (mosquitto) and MQTT integration

We watched the provided youtube video and learned the basics of MQTT and especially the Mosquitto Broker in practise. 

The main CLI commands in the YT Video were *mosquitto_sub* to subscribe to a topic and *mosquitto_pub* to publish data to a particular topic.

**Standout Information:**

- It is interesting to see, that the Topic does not need to be defined within the MQTT Broker.
- We were able to publish and subscribe to any topic we wanted.
- It seems, that the Mosquitto Broker is pretty open when it comes to who is allowed to perform certain tasks. 
- There was no restriction to who can subscribe and who can publish data.

### Extension - Write to another topic

For this part of the exercise, we had to adjust both our scripts. We changed the publisher-script to send data representing temperature in a loop. We made the temperatures iterate between 15 and 30 degrees with a little jitter. 

This script can be found [here](). 

The subscriber-script takes those values, converts them to float variables and checks whether a certain limit is reached. If for example the temperature is below 20 degrees, the subscriber-script publishes to a second topic, that the heating should be turned off. The same logic applies to the scenario when the heating should be turned on, when the temperature is above a certain threshold. 

We used this hysteresis to allow two different threshold define the behaviour of the AC. This allows more reliable, realisitic and robust heating. Using no hysterisis would make the AC switch on and off very fast, when the temperature is near the target temperature of the AC.
